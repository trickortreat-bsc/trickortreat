Questions

    
What exactly is a “treat”?
One treat = one share = 1,000,000 TREAT tokens, sent to your wallet the moment you claim or buy. TREAT is a plain ERC-20 on BNB Chain: no transfer tax, no admin, no one can mint more.

    
Why must I post before the free claim?
The post is the knock on the door — it is how the free treats find new people. A small gate service checks that your post carries this site's link and your wallet code, then signs a one-time permit the contract verifies. Paid treats skip all of that.

    
Can I claim the free treat twice?
No. One per wallet and one per X account, enforced by the contract itself.

    
Where does the money go?
100% of every paid purchase goes straight to the project treasury, inside the same transaction. Nothing is skimmed by the contract.

    
When does the sale end?
When the 5,000th treat leaves the bucket. There is no timer.
